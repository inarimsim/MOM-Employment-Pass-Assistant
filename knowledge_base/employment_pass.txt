# EMPLOYMENT PASS (EP)

## Overview

The Employment Pass (EP) is for foreign professionals, managers and executives who have a job offer in Singapore and meet the eligibility requirements.

An employer or appointed employment agent must apply on behalf of the candidate. For overseas companies without a Singapore-registered office, a local sponsor is required to submit the application.

There is no foreign worker levy or quota required for Employment Pass holders.

Eligible family members may apply for family-related passes.

Employment Pass holders may be subject to Singapore income tax requirements.

## Key Facts

Who the pass is for:
- Foreign professionals
- Managers
- Executives

Qualifying salary:
- Fixed monthly salary starting from SGD 5,600
- Salary requirement increases progressively with age
- Can reach approximately SGD 10,700 for applicants in their mid-40s
- Candidates in the financial services sector may require higher qualifying salaries

Pass duration:
- First-time applicants: Up to 2 years
- Renewals: Up to 3 years

Experienced technology professionals with shortage skills may qualify for a 5-year Employment Pass.

Medical insurance:
- Employers may choose whether to provide medical insurance for Employment Pass holders.

## Family Benefits

Employment Pass holders may be eligible to bring family members to Singapore through available family pass schemes, subject to MOM eligibility requirements.

## Fair Consideration Framework (FCF)

Before submitting an Employment Pass application, employers are generally required to advertise the vacancy on MyCareersFuture.

The objective of the Fair Consideration Framework (FCF) is to:
- Promote fair hiring
- Improve labour market transparency
- Encourage employers to consider all candidates fairly

Employers should not discriminate against candidates based on:
- Age
- Sex
- Nationality
- Race
- Other non-job-related characteristics

Employers are expected to comply with the Tripartite Guidelines on Fair Employment Practices.

## Job Advertisement Requirements

Employment Pass applications may be rejected if the related advertisement:

- Contains discriminatory wording
- Does not accurately represent the role
- Uses a different occupation from the EP application
- Is submitted by a different employer than the advertiser
- Contains salary information that is unclear or misleading

Salary advertisement rules:

- Salary range must be visible
- Salary range cannot be excessively broad
- Maximum salary should not exceed double the minimum salary
- Advertised salary must include the salary intended for the EP candidate

Where a single advertisement is used for multiple EP applications, the number of applications cannot exceed the number of advertised vacancies.

## Advertising Duration Requirements

The MyCareersFuture job advertisement must:

- Be open for at least 14 consecutive days
- Allow sufficient time for jobseekers to apply
- Be reposted if key details are changed

Changes that require a new advertisement include:
- Organisation identifier
- Occupation
- Salary
- Number of vacancies

Advertisements that have expired or been closed for more than 3 months cannot be used for Employment Pass applications.

## Additional Fair Hiring Requirements

Employers should not make a job offer during the required 14-day advertising period.

This allows:
- Fair consideration of candidates
- Sufficient application time for jobseekers

## Exemptions from Advertising Requirements

Employers may be exempt from MyCareersFuture advertising if any of the following apply:

- Company has fewer than 10 employees
- Fixed monthly salary is SGD 22,500 or higher
- Job duration is 1 month or less
- Position is filled by a local transferee

Examples of exempt situations include:
- Internal transfers between related offices
- Eligible overseas intra-corporate transferees

## Application Process

Before applying:

1. Advertise on MyCareersFuture where required.
2. Consider all candidates fairly.
3. Verify Employment Pass eligibility requirements.
4. Prepare required supporting documents.
5. Submit application through the appropriate MOM system.

## Renewal

Employment Passes can be renewed if renewal requirements are met.

Renewed passes may be issued for up to 3 years.

## Relevant Legislation

- Employment of Foreign Manpower Act
- Conditions of Work Pass

## Employment Pass Eligibility

Employment Pass (EP) applications follow a 2-stage eligibility framework.

Stage 1: Qualifying Salary

Candidates must meet the Employment Pass qualifying salary requirements.

Current qualifying salary:

Non-financial services sector:
- Minimum SGD 5,600 per month
- Increases with age
- Up to approximately SGD 10,700 for applicants aged 45 and above

Financial services sector:
- Minimum SGD 6,200 per month
- Increases with age
- Up to approximately SGD 11,800 for applicants aged 45 and above

Future qualifying salary requirements:

Non-financial services:
- SGD 6,000 minimum
- Up to SGD 11,500 for older applicants

Financial services:
- SGD 6,600 minimum
- Up to SGD 12,700 for older applicants

Candidates who do not meet the qualifying salary requirement will not be eligible for Employment Pass regardless of their COMPASS score.

Employers can use the Self-Assessment Tool (SAT) before submitting an application.

## COMPASS Framework

The Complementarity Assessment Framework (COMPASS) is a points-based assessment framework used for Employment Pass applications.

Candidates generally need:
- Minimum of 40 points to pass COMPASS.

COMPASS evaluates both candidate-level and company-level factors.

### Criterion C1 – Salary

Points awarded based on candidate salary compared to local PMET salaries in the sector.

- 90th percentile and above: 20 points
- 65th to below 90th percentile: 10 points
- Below 65th percentile: 0 points

### Criterion C2 – Qualifications

Top-tier degree-equivalent qualifications:
- 20 points

Other degree-equivalent qualifications:
- 10 points

No degree-equivalent qualifications:
- 0 points

Candidates without degree-equivalent qualifications may still pass COMPASS if they accumulate enough points from other criteria.

### Criterion C3 – Diversity

Measures the concentration of the candidate's nationality within the employer's PMET workforce.

Fewer than 25 PMETs:
- Automatic 10 points

For organisations with 25 or more PMETs:

Nationality share below 5%:
- 20 points

Nationality share between 5% and below 25%:
- 10 points

Nationality share 25% or higher:
- 0 points

### Criterion C4 – Support for Local Employment

Measures the organisation's local PMET representation.

Fewer than 25 PMETs:
- Automatic 10 points

50th percentile and above:
- 20 points

20th to below 50th percentile:
- 10 points

Below 20th percentile:
- 0 points

### Criterion C5 – Skills Bonus

Applies to occupations on the Shortage Occupation List (SOL).

Job on SOL and nationality share less than one-third:
- 20 bonus points

Job on SOL and nationality share one-third or more:
- 10 bonus points

Additional eligibility checks may be required.

### Criterion C6 – Strategic Economic Priorities (SEP) Bonus

Employers participating in recognised economic programmes may receive:

- 10 bonus points

These bonus points are valid for up to 3 years.

## COMPASS Exemptions

Candidates may be exempt from COMPASS if they satisfy any of these conditions:

- Fixed monthly salary of at least SGD 22,500
- Overseas intra-corporate transferee
- Short-term role of 1 month or less

## Family Passes

Employment Pass holders can bring family members to Singapore if:

- Holder earns at least SGD 6,000 per month
- Holder possesses a valid Employment Pass

Eligible family members:

Dependant's Pass:
- Legally married spouse
- Unmarried children below 21 years old
- Adopted children below 21 years old

Long-Term Visit Pass:
- Common-law spouse
- Unmarried handicapped children aged 21 and above
- Unmarried stepchildren below 21 years old

Parents:
- Eligible under Long-Term Visit Pass
- EP holder must earn at least SGD 12,000 per month

## Documents Required

Required documents generally include:

- Passport personal particulars page
- Company ACRA profile
- Qualification verification documents (if applicable)

Additional supporting documents may be required for regulated professions such as:
- Doctors
- Dentists
- Nurses
- Pharmacists
- Lawyers
- Allied health professionals

Non-English documents must be submitted together with an English translation.

## Employment Pass Application Process

Step 1
Obtain candidate's written consent.

Step 2
Complete candidate information form.

Step 3
Submit application through EP eService.

Step 4
Upload required supporting documents.

Step 5
Pay SGD 105 application fee.

Processing time:
- Typically within 10 business days

If approved:

- Candidate receives In-Principle Approval (IPA)
- IPA valid for 6 months
- Candidate travels to Singapore
- Pass is issued
- Digital work pass is activated
- Card delivered within approximately 5 working days after registration

Issuance fees:
- SGD 225 per pass
- SGD 30 Multiple Journey Visa (if applicable)

## Fair Consideration Framework (FCF)

Employers applying for Employment Passes must generally:

- Advertise roles on MyCareersFuture
- Keep advertisements open for at least 14 consecutive days
- Consider candidates fairly

Advertisements must:
- Be accurate
- Be non-discriminatory
- Include salary information
- Match the submitted EP application

Employers should not make job offers during the mandatory advertising period.

Advertising exemptions apply for:

- Companies with fewer than 10 employees
- Roles paying at least SGD 22,500 monthly
- Roles of one month or less
- Local transferees
- Eligible overseas intra-corporate transferees

## Appeal Against Rejected Employment Pass Applications

Employers may appeal a rejected Employment Pass application within 3 months of rejection.

Appeals should only be submitted if:
- The reasons for rejection can be addressed.
- New information can be provided.

Employers should:
- Review the rejection advisory.
- Use the Self-Assessment Tool (SAT) to reassess eligibility.

Appeals are submitted through EP eService.

Supporting documents should only be submitted when necessary.

Typical appeal processing time:
- 85% of appeals are processed within 6 weeks.

Only the employer or authorised representative who submitted the application can appeal.

## Employment Pass Renewal

An Employment Pass may be renewed if:
- The pass holder continues working for the same employer.
- The pass holder remains in a managerial, executive or specialised role.
- Salary requirements continue to be met.
- COMPASS requirements are satisfied where applicable.

Renewal timeline:
- Up to 6 months before expiry.
- EP Sponsorship cases: up to 3 months before expiry.

Renewed pass validity:
- Up to 3 years.

Renewal fees:
- SGD 225 issuance fee.
- SGD 30 Multiple Journey Visa (if applicable).

Renewals are not guaranteed.

Processing:
- Usually within 10 business days.

## Cancellation of Employment Pass

Employers must cancel the Employment Pass when employment ends.

Cancellation timeline:
- Within 1 week after the final day of notice.

Cancellation effects:
- Pass holder can no longer work.
- Related family passes are automatically cancelled.
- Work pass card should be destroyed.

Employers should:
- Obtain tax clearance from IRAS.
- Settle outstanding employment issues.
- Arrange repatriation if applicable.

A Short-Term Visit Pass (STVP) may be requested to allow temporary stay in Singapore after cancellation.

## Replacement of Employment Pass Card

Replacement must be requested within 1 week if the card is:
- Lost
- Stolen
- Damaged

Fees:

Damaged card:
- SGD 65.40

Lost or stolen card:
- First replacement: SGD 109
- Subsequent replacements: SGD 327

Replacement requests are typically processed immediately.

New card collection:
- Usually within 4 working days.

For stolen cards:
- Police report required.

## Updates That Must Be Reported to MOM

Employers or pass holders must notify MOM regarding relevant changes.

Examples:

Salary changes:
- Salary reductions must be reported at least 1 month before implementation.
- Salary increases generally updated during renewal unless dependant privileges are being requested.

Company changes:
- Company name
- Paid-up capital
- Company address
- Business restructuring

Pass holder updates:
- Residential address
- Mobile number
- Passport details
- Nationality
- Marital status
- Occupation

Residential addresses and mobile numbers should generally be updated within 5 days.

## 5-Year Employment Pass for Experienced Technology Professionals

Eligible technology professionals may qualify for a 5-year Employment Pass.

Requirements include:
- Occupation listed on the COMPASS Shortage Occupation List (SOL).
- Successful COMPASS assessment.
- Meeting additional salary thresholds.
- Satisfying occupation-specific verification requirements.

This longer-duration Employment Pass aims to support highly skilled technology professionals working in shortage occupations.
